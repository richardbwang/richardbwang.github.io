01Language: C++

RW-Template

  • 83 stars
  • 25 forks
  • 1,000+ release downloads
  • as of Oct 2026

VEX V5 autonomous template for VEXcode and VS Code: PID and odometry (with or without tracking wheels), Monte Carlo Localization, motion profiling, pure pursuit and RAMSETE path following, and a simulator that fits a drive model from a 15-second calibration log, so you can test autons without a robot.

In development since June 2022 and open source since May 2025, with a 450+ member community around it.

Also runs on PROS. X-drive, mecanum and H-drive; async motions; an auton selector.

02Language: Python

drone-perception

Autonomous drone perception, built at a South Park Commons hackathon (May 2026). YOLO spots the Coke cans in real time; the drone estimates where they are, turns, flies in on its own and knocks them over. Vision inference, telemetry and relative waypoint control run through NimbusOS.

  • Python
  • YOLO
  • NimbusOS

03Language: TypeScript

toph

Farm activity dashboard with hands-free voice logs. A worker’s phone reads five questions aloud and transcribes the answers; the manager reviews each log on a live desktop dashboard with waveform playback, tags, review status and a satellite map of the field. Built full-stack, with session auth, from a Figma design for LavaLab’s Fall 2026 developer challenge.

duesync

USC coursework deadlines, synced to Google Calendar. Every 10 minutes, on Google’s servers, it pulls due dates from Gradescope and Brightspace and has Claude read emails and syllabus PDFs for exams and deadlines. Bigger deadlines get earlier, escalating reminders and countdown banners, and a morning digest lists what’s due.

Papers

Monocular vision that holds up when the camera or the conditions don’t. First author on both.

conditions · knocked camera · darkness · dim light · glare

  1. IEEE ICCAS 2025Camera misalignment

    A Bias-Aware Learning Model for Reliable Monocular Perception under Camera Misalignment

    authors
    Richard B. Wang, Grant Han, Alexander Liu, Zixuan Ye, Alexander Deng, Lexie Liu, Xing Wei*
    venue
    Proc. IEEE International Conference on Control, Automation and Systems (ICCAS), 2025
    pages
    1643–1649
    tl;dr
    A lightweight bias-modeling module that learns and compensates for the systematic errors that appear when a robot’s camera gets knocked out of alignment. Evaluated on monocular distance estimation.

  2. IEEE WRC SARA 2025Darkness · dim light · glare
